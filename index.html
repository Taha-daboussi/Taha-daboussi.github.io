01 — About

How I work

RoleBackend engineer
FocusTrading infrastructure
CoreGo · Postgres · Kafka
BaseTripoli → remote, US

I'm the engineer who takes the part of the system that isn't allowed to be wrong — the matching engine, the ledger, the data path — and makes it boring. On a trading backend that's where a silent state divergence quietly costs real money, so I tend to own those pieces end to end: the design, the Postgres transaction boundaries, the Kafka partitioning, and the observability that tells you the moment something starts to drift.

I work best on small teams where I can hold the whole pipeline in my head and still hand other people clean seams to build against. At AIY I owned hiring and technical direction for a four-person team; at Distribute AI I took a central limit order book and trading terminal from architecture to shipped. I'm self-taught, and it shows in what I value: I started coding at fifteen, shipped paid products by nineteen, and sold a company by twenty-three. I'd rather ship a narrow thing that survives production traffic than a broad thing that demos well — TridentKit ran real money for two and a half years before it was acquired, and the Solana market-data pipeline I built was measured 80–120 ms ahead of its named competitors because, there, latency was the product.

02 — Experience

Where I've shipped

  1. Oct 2025 – May 2026 · Remote · San Francisco, USA

    Backend Engineer, Trading Infrastructure

    Distribute AI

    • Architected a Go central limit order book and matching engine for a binary-asset prediction market on a Simulate→Persist→Apply pipeline — order reservations, fill events, and outbox rows committed in a single Postgres transaction before any in-memory state mutates, making ledger/state divergence structurally impossible.
    • Scaled the engine across 8 Kubernetes pods and 64 shards via co-partitioned Kafka topics with partition-pinned ownership keyed by book ID; 3K–5K req/sec sustained with 5–10× burst absorption and OnRevoked snapshot-and-evict rebalance semantics.
    • Engineered a double-entry settlement ledger with four settlement paths plus a transactional outbox and drainer; bulk COPY FROM writes collapsed per-trade I/O and decoupled write-path availability from Kafka.
